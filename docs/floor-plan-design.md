# Courtyard Residence — Floor Plan Design Note

## Overview & Programmatic Intent

This schematic architectural design defines a single-storey courtyard residence in Dallas, Texas, updating the conceptual plan in response to direct client feedback (Feedback 1 reference pages 1–3 and live design revisions). The programmatic brief establishes a balanced, daylight-filled layout wrapping a central outdoor courtyard with clear spatial zones, generous circulation, and natural indoor-outdoor connectivity.

### Key Programmatic Allocations
- **Northwest (Upper-Left)**: Expanded Working Kitchen with narrowed walk-in Pantry ($3'-6" \times 8'-6"$), a west-wall prep countertop ($2'-0" \times 3'-9"$, $X \in [4.75, 6.75]$, $Y \in [2.75, 6.5]$) that stops short of the relocated pantry pocket door ($Y \in [6.625, 9.295]$), and a generous central island ($6'-6" \times 3'-3"$ at $X \in [10.25, 16.75]$, $Y \in [6.75, 10.0]$) with the prep sink centered on it and three evenly spaced counter stools along its south edge ($26\text{ in}$ nominal frontage per seat, centers at $X = 11.4, 13.5, 15.6$). Measured work aisles: $42\text{ in}$ ($3.5\text{ ft}$) between the west counter face and the island, $48\text{ in}$ ($4.0\text{ ft}$) between the north counter / range front and the island, $36\text{ in}$ ($3.0\text{ ft}$) open circulation between the island and the east laundry wall (no east work counter), and $>2\text{ ft}$ of stool pull-out behind the seats before the open dining passage. A direct passage door on the east wall provides immediate access into the Laundry / Utility room without traversing public circulation or bedrooms.
- **West / Mid-Left**: Dining Room in open concept with Kitchen and Living Room, featuring an 8-ft picture window overlooking the west garden. Furnished with an enlarged dining table ($8'-0" \times 3'-7"$) seating eight guests, complete with eight drawn chairs (three north, three south, one west captain, one east captain) and generous circulation ($\ge 2.25\text{ ft}$ west, $3.1\text{ ft}$ north, $4.0\text{ ft}$ south, and $>5.5\text{ ft}$ east).
- **Southwest (Lower-Left)**: Living Room entered from the South exterior front door and coat closet foyer, open to dining, and connected to the central courtyard through a 7-ft sliding glass wall. The furniture grouping is shifted south toward the front wall, anchored by a generous $11'-0" \times 13'-0"$ area rug, a coffee table ($4'-0" \times 2'-5"$) centered geometrically on the rug, a proportioned sectional sofa ($9'-0" \times 7'-6"$), two comfortable armchairs, and a dedicated credenza ($5'-6" \times 1'-7"$) placed along the solid south front-door wall, completely clear of the entry door swing and travel path.
- **North Center (Second Guest Bedroom)**: The former north gallery has been transformed into a fully private Second Guest Bedroom ($10'-0" \times 12'-9"$, $127.5\text{ sq ft}$), meeting all IRC habitable space standards. Features direct, independent entrance from the continuous Shared Hallway, an operable casement emergency escape window on the North exterior wall, a queen bed with nightstand, and a dedicated built-in closet with interior hanging rod.
- **South Center**: Repurposed former dining space converted into a quiet Hallway / Sitting Area and reading lounge, featuring a 12-ft sliding glass wall opening north into the central courtyard.
- **Central Courtyard**: An open-to-the-sky courtyard ($21'-0" \times 23'-0"$, $483\text{ sq ft}$) featuring an architectural specimen tree with natural clustered foliage canopy, varied forking branch structure, subtle layered green transparencies, and delicate leaf-like accents (following Feedback 1 page 2), perimeter stone terrace paving, an understated fountain, and clear egress paths.
- **East Wing (Primary Suite & Guest Bedroom 1)**: Both the Primary Suite and Guest Bedroom 1 are situated along the exterior East wall with direct access to the continuous $4.25\text{ ft}$ wide Shared Hallway:
  - **Primary Bedroom Suite**: Directly reinstating the authentic Feedback 3 reference layout (`../review-3/page-2-image-1.png` and `page-2.png`), the primary suite is organized into three linked spaces along the east wing:
    - **Primary Bedroom** ($16'-0" \times 10'-9"$, $172.0\text{ sq ft}$, $Y \in [17.0, 27.75]$): Entered directly from the continuous Shared Hallway via a nominal 36-in door (`OP_PRI_BED_DOOR`, $Y \in [24.375, 27.375]$) at the south end, swinging against the fixture-free south wall. The king bed ($6'-6" \times 6'-10"$, $X \in [54.0, 60.5]$) with dual flanking nightstands occupies the east half of the north wall, leaving $3.75\text{ ft}$ of foot clearance to the south wall and a $4.5\text{ ft}$ wide open axis along the west side of the room. The dresser ($1'-10" \times 3'-8"$) is pushed flat against the left (west) wall at $Y \in [19.8, 23.5]$, clear of the door swing quadrant. The operable casement emergency escape window ($Y \in [23.5, 27.5]$) on the exterior east wall discharges directly to the East Yard from the open foot-of-bed zone. A private pocket door (`OP_PRI_BED_TO_DRESS`) on the west circulation axis ($X \in [48.0, 51.0]$) leads directly into the dressing room; no door crosses any furniture.
    - **Primary Dressing Room** ($16'-0" \times 6'-9"$, $108.0\text{ sq ft}$, $Y \in [9.75, 16.5]$): A central makeup / dressing table (`FURN_PRI_DRESSING_TABLE`, $5'-6" \times 1'-6"$) with a round vanity stool directly backs the bathroom's double vanity divider. The $11'-6"$ continuous built-in wardrobe run (`FIX_PRI_WARDROBE_RUN`, $2'-0"$ deep with sliding doors and interior hanging rod) runs along the solid south wall at $X \in [51.5, 63.0]$, east of a $4.25\text{ ft}$ wide open west axis ($X \in [47.25, 51.5]$) that connects the bedroom pocket door straight through to the $3'-0"$ cased opening (`OP_PRI_DRESS_TO_BATH`, $X \in [48.0, 51.0]$) into the bath. Wardrobe approach depth is $4.75\text{ ft}$ at both ends of the run and $3.25\text{ ft}$ between the table edge and the wardrobe face in the middle (stool is movable); the suite path never crosses the stool.
    - **Primary Bathroom** ($16'-0" \times 8'-6"$, $136.0\text{ sq ft}$, $Y \in [0.75, 9.25]$): Deepened by $2.0\text{ ft}$ while replicating the Feedback 3 arrangement: walk-in shower ($4'-6" \times 4'-0"$) in the upper-left, central oval freestanding soaking tub ($5'-6" \times 2'-10"$, front edge at $Y = 3.65$) set against an exterior north garden window, private water closet ($2'-6" \times 2'-6"$) in the upper-right, and a central double vanity ($5'-6" \times 2'-0"$, front edge at $Y = 7.25$) on the south wall facing north toward the tub, flanked by built-in storage cabinetry. Clear floor between the tub front and the facing vanity is $43.2\text{ in}$ ($3.6\text{ ft}$).
  - **Guest Bedroom 1**: Located in the southeast corner ($16'-0" \times 17'-0"$, $272\text{ sq ft}$), featuring a queen bed, two nightstands, a dedicated built-in closet with dual doors and interior hanging rod, a study desk, direct hallway access, and an operable exterior east escape window.
- **Guest Bathroom**: Re-planned full bathroom in the east bay ($16'-0" \times 7'-6"$) featuring an end-to-end full-depth tub surround ($3'-3" \times 7'-6"$) spanning the entire right/east wall with a centered sensible bath basin and tiled surround ledges; the entrance door is reversed to the south corner of the west wall, opening smoothly against a completely fixture-free south wall; and the single vanity ($4'-6"$) and water closet are positioned along the north wall with $>30\text{ in}$ centerline spacing and $>5\text{ ft}$ ($60\text{ in}$) clear floor space in front. 0 door collisions.
- **Accessibility & Circulation**: Single-storey construction throughout with zero stairs, generous circulation routes ($4.0\text{ ft}$ target clear width, narrowing appropriately only at doorway openings), and nominal 36-in doors.

## Model Conventions & Schema Architecture

The editable data model in [`assets/brand/floor-plan-model.json`](../assets/brand/floor-plan-model.json) adheres to a simple, compact JSON schema designed to allow deterministic SVG rendering:

1. **Coordinate System & Outer Envelope**:
   - World units are explicit decimal **feet**.
   - Outer boundary envelope spans exactly `(0.0, 0.0)` to `(64.0, 54.0)`.
   - Positive X extends East; positive Y extends South.
   - Exterior wall centerlines are offset inward by half their thickness: North Y=0.375, South Y=53.625, West X=0.375, East X=63.625 (thickness 0.75 ft).
2. **Drawing Output Specification**:
   - Target SVG `viewBox`: `0 0 1600 1200`.
   - Scale factor: `16.0 pixels per foot` (`64 ft × 16 = 1024 px` width; `54 ft × 16 = 864 px` height).
   - Centering offset: `X = 288.0 px`, `Y = 168.0 px`.
   - Generous margins accommodate exterior landscaping, East Yard egress pathway, South street sidewalk, stepping stones, and dimension annotations.
   - Color palette maintains warm cream paper (`#f5f2e9`), dark sage outer walls (`#3b5e3a`), medium sage partitions (`#4d734c`), soft green fixtures/furniture (`#5f8156`), and layered translucent foliage (`#e2e8ce` and `#d5e0be` with `#4d734c` outlines).
3. **Wall Topology & Partitions**:
   - Walls are modeled as horizontal/vertical centerline segments between discrete endpoints with explicit thickness: exterior walls `0.75 ft` (9 in); plumbing walls `0.50 ft` (6 in); interior partitions `0.375 ft` (4.5 in).
   - Kitchen and laundry are separated by partition `W_INT_KIT_LAUND` along X=20 (Y: 0.375 to 14.0) with direct access door `OP_LAUNDRY_DOOR`.
   - Living room and sitting area are connected through partition `W_INT_LIV_SIT` along X=20 (Y: 38.0 to 53.625) featuring an 8-ft cased opening (`OP_LIVING_TO_SITTING`). South exterior wall remains solid.
   - Second guest bedroom is partitioned from laundry by `W_INT_BED2_W` at X=31.375 and from the shared hallway by `W_INT_BED2_E` at X=42.0.
   - Shared Hallway runs continuously along X=42.5 to 46.75 from Y=0.75 down to Y=53.0.
   - Primary suite is partitioned into its authentic Feedback 3 sequence by plumbing divider `W_INT_PRI_DIV1` (thickness 0.5 ft at Y=9.5) and partition divider `W_INT_PRI_DIV2` (thickness 0.5 ft at Y=16.75); the bath band is 8.5 ft deep, the dressing band 6.75 ft, and the bedroom 10.75 ft within the 27-ft primary strip.
4. **Openings, Doors & Clearances**:
   - Openings are indexed to host wall IDs with linear offset, width, height, swing type, and connected spaces.
   - Door swing arcs rotate into clear floor space without colliding with adjacent casework or passage lines.
   - Primary bedroom entrance door (`OP_PRI_BED_DOOR`) is positioned at the south end of the room ($Y \in [24.375, 27.375]$), swinging smoothly against the fixture-free south wall with its swing quadrant ($X \in [47.25, 50.25]$) clear of the dresser (ends $Y=23.5$) and bed.
   - Primary dressing room connects to primary bedroom via pocket door `OP_PRI_BED_TO_DRESS` on the west circulation axis ($X \in [48.0, 51.0]$), aligned directly under the $3.0\text{-ft}$ cased opening `OP_PRI_DRESS_TO_BATH` into the primary bathroom ($X \in [48.0, 51.0]$), so the bedroom-to-bath path is a straight run that never crosses the dressing stool or wardrobe approach.
   - Pantry pocket door (`OP_PANTRY_DOOR`, $Y \in [6.625, 9.295]$) sits south of the west prep counter (ends $Y=6.5$) so the door is not blocked by casework.
   - Guest bath door swings south against a fixture-free wall, leaving $>2.3\text{ ft}$ clearance to the north vanity.
   - Nominal 36-in doors assumed to provide required >= 32-in net clear width upon product selection.
5. **Fixtures & Furniture Containment**:
   - Every fixture and furniture item is strictly contained within the clear rectangle of its assigned space.
   - Guest bathroom full-depth tub surround ($3.25\text{ ft} \times 7.5\text{ ft}$) spans end-to-end against the east wall ($X \in [60.0, 63.25], Y \in [28.25, 35.75]$).
   - Island is sized to $6.5' \times 3.25'$ at $X \in [10.25, 16.75]$, seating three evenly spaced counter stools along its south side ($26\text{ in}$ nominal frontage each) with a $42\text{-in}$ west aisle, $48\text{-in}$ north aisle, and $36\text{-in}$ east circulation.
   - Primary dressing room features an $11.5\text{-ft}$ long continuous sliding-door wardrobe run (`FIX_PRI_WARDROBE_RUN`, $X \in [51.5, 63.0]$) with interior hanging rod along the solid south wall, and a makeup table with stool backing the central vanity; the west $4.25\text{-ft}$ axis is kept clear for the bedroom-to-bath path.
   - Primary bath keeps $43.2\text{ in}$ of clear floor between the freestanding tub front ($Y=3.65$) and the facing double vanity ($Y=7.25$).
   - Primary bedroom dresser is pushed flat against the left (west) wall with circular element removed, clear of the door swing.
6. **Circulation Routes**:
   - All 9 circulation polylines follow genuine open travel corridors:
     - Main entry to Primary Bedroom traverses open living/sitting corridors and enters bedroom at Y=25.5.
     - Main entry to Guest Bedroom 1 enters bedroom at Y=39.5.
     - Main entry to Second Guest Bedroom enters bedroom at Y=3.5.
     - Living to kitchen route runs north at X=17.5 through the east circulation, turns west at Y=12.6 behind the island stools, north up the 42-in west aisle at X=8.5, and enters the pantry pocket door at Y=7.95.
     - Primary bedroom to bath route runs from the hall door along the open west axis (X=50.5 then 49.5) through the dressing pocket door and the cased opening into the bath.
     - Living to courtyard route passes through 7-ft slider opening.
     - Bedroom egress routes originate in open floor space and pass through exterior casement windows into yards (Primary egress connects from Y=25.5 directly to the east escape window).
7. **Architectural Trees**:
   - Realistic top-down architectural foliage generator: organic irregular clustered canopy outlines with cubic bezier curves, secondary layered foliage washes with subtle green transparencies, interior cluster discs, radiating limbs branching into secondary forking twigs, and delicate leaf accents following Feedback 1 page 2.
   - All 8 trees fit within the 1600×1200 canvas viewBox.

## Code Basis & Planning Assumptions (Dallas, TX)

The design layout incorporates spatial dimensions derived from the City of Dallas residential building regulations:

### Regulatory Framework
- **Base Code**: 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), verified via municipal references.
- **Local Amendments**: City of Dallas Ordinance 23-0471 (Dallas City Code Chapter 57 amendments to the 2021 IRC, effective May 12, 2023) and Ordinance 33099 (administrative updates).

### Key Planning Provisions Referenced
1. **Habitable Space Standards (IRC R304.1–R304.2)**:
   - Every habitable room provides well over the 70 sq ft minimum floor area and exceeds the 7-ft minimum horizontal dimension (minimum habitable room dimension in design is 10'-0" in Second Guest Bedroom).
2. **Ceiling Height Assumptions (IRC R305.1)**:
   - Schematic design assumes 10'-0" clear ceilings in living, dining, kitchen, and primary bedroom; 9'-6" in sitting and guest bedrooms; and 9'-0" in bathrooms, hallways, closets, and service spaces. All exceed the 7'-0" (habitable) and 6'-8" (bathrooms/laundry) baseline minimums.
3. **Emergency Escape and Rescue Openings (IRC R310.1–R310.2 & Dallas Amendments)**:
   - Dallas 2021 IRC Section R310 requires sleeping rooms to have operable escape and rescue openings discharging directly to a public way or to a yard/court having an unobstructed path to a public way.
   - **Crucial Layout Decision**: All three bedrooms (Primary, Guest Bedroom 1, and Second Guest Bedroom) are located on exterior walls with dedicated operable casement windows (`OP_PRI_EGRESS_WINDOW`, `OP_GUEST_EGRESS_WINDOW`, `OP_GUEST2_EGRESS_WINDOW`) discharging directly to the exterior East and North yards.
   - The East and North yards maintain unobstructed $\ge 4'-0"$ wide exterior pathways leading directly to the front public street. The bedrooms do not rely upon an enclosed courtyard for emergency egress.
   - **Assumed Window Clearances**: Casement window openings assume a net clear width >= 26 in, net clear height >= 48 in, net clear area >= 8.67 sq ft (exceeding the 5.7 sq ft IRC minimum / 5.0 sq ft grade floor requirement), with sill height at 32 in above finished floor (below the 44 in maximum). Net clear dimensions remain design assumptions requiring manufacturer product verification. Exterior public-way routing is a site planning assumption.
4. **Means of Egress & Doors (IRC R311.2–R311.6)**:
   - Primary exterior egress door (`OP_FRONT_ENTRY`) is a side-hinged nominal 36-in door assumed to provide at least 32 in net clear opening width and 78 in height. Exterior landing depth exceeds 36 in.
   - Interior passage doors are nominal 36 in (3.0 ft) throughout for generous universal circulation, narrowing appropriately at doorways.
   - Main circulation corridors provide 4.25 ft to 5.0 ft clear width, exceeding the 36-in code minimum.
5. **Bathroom Clearances (IRC R307 & Dallas Amendments)**:
   - Plumbing fixtures maintain greater than 30 in centerline spacing and over 24 in clear in front of water closets and lavatories. Dallas toilet-wall blocking provisions (R307.4) noted for structural detailing.

### Scope & Limits of Validation
Exact limits: Geometric verification utilizes 2D bounding-box containment, room rectangle partition checks, door leaf and swept quadrant sector checks, and centerline ray-segment intersection testing for walls, openings, and furnishings. Full CAD 3D volumetric clearance, corner-turn swing physics, ADA turning circle simulations, and manufacturer-specific rough opening tolerances are not performed.
